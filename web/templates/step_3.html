Max Count

A numeric value, a multiple of 25, with a minimum of 25 and a maximum of 250, that limits the total number of results to be returned. The default is 125.

e.g. 50

Similarity Threshold

A numeric value, with a minimum of 0 and a maximum of 100, used as a threshold to filter and remove articles with similar titles by the same authors. The default is 80.

e.g. 80
