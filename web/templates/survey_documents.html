STEP 4 - Survey documents

Finally, all fields are submitted to document survey, and their data will be systematically collected, validated, and refined, removing duplicates and filtering similar documents, leaving only the most relevant and recent data.

Max Count Optional

A numeric value, a multiple of 25, with a minimum of 25 and a maximum of 250, that limits the total number of results to be returned. The default is 125.

e.g. 50

Similarity Threshold Optional

The threshold value, in the range of 0 to 100, used to filter documents with the same author(s) and similar titles, keeping the one with the most recent publication date. Default to 80.

e.g. 80
