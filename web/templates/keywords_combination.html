STEP 3 - Keywords combination

Based on the topic or subject of your research, you must select the keywords that will be used to search in the abstracts, keywords, and titles, and find documents that contain them all. Using the combined field TITLE-ABS-KEY, we survey data and the total of documents found in Scopus for each combination of keywords concatenated by the Boolean operator AND.

Keywords Required

The keywords, with a minimum of two (required) and a maximum of four, that the documents you are searching contain. They must be written in English, with a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 70 characters. They can contain letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens, underscores, phrases, wildcards, and the Boolean operators OR and AND NOT. Because AND NOT can generate unexpected results, it should be used in the last field.
